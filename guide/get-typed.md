# Get typed

verified: 2026-10-19. Navigation, not medical advice: this page says what tests exist and how to get them, not which one a person should have. Prices and availability are US unless stated and move fast. Re-verify anything older than 90 days.

## Why bother

No approved treatment today depends on subtype. What subtype changes right now: which disease-modifying trials a person can enter, candidacy and expected benefit from deep brain stimulation or focused ultrasound, how closely to watch cognition and blood pressure, which drugs to avoid, and whether a diagnosis deserves a second look. The honest pitch is participation in the answer, not receipt of one.

A note on genetic results: they carry information about blood relatives. Programs that bundle counseling make that easier to handle. Which route a person chooses is their decision.

## Recommended order for most people

1. **Ask for the clinical motor subtype.** Costs nothing, and it is the axis with the most immediate treatment implications.
2. **Get the gene panel.** PD GENEration in the Americas and Israel; NHS route or PD Frontline in the UK; a clinician-ordered panel elsewhere. About 13 percent come back positive. Prior testing elsewhere does not exclude you from PD GENEration.
3. **Join an observational cohort** before any interventional trial. PPMI provides the synuclein assay and DaTscan at no cost inside the study. See `on-ramps.md`.
4. **Consider the synuclein assay** on its own only if there is diagnostic doubt, the person is a LRRK2 carrier (about a third are negative), or a specific trial requires it.

## Test details

### Clinical motor subtype

Computed from the ratio of tremor items to postural instability and gait items on the MDS-UPDRS. Any neurologist can report it. Tremor-dominant progresses more slowly and responds well to deep brain stimulation and focused ultrasound (Exablate Neuro approved for tremor-dominant PD in 2018). Gait-dominant progresses faster with more falls and cognitive decline; freezing of gait responds poorly to stimulation and dopaminergic drugs, and structured exercise is the best-evidenced intervention. Source: corpus file 04.

### PD GENEration (Parkinson's Foundation)

- Eligibility: confirmed Parkinson's diagnosis, age 18 or older. People already tested elsewhere are encouraged to join.
- Method: whole genome sequencing at a CAP/CLIA lab, variants curated for the seven-gene primary panel. Some reports describe secondary findings in 21 PD-related genes.
- Geography: at-home kit (Tasso+ blood collection) in the US, Canada, Puerto Rico and Dominican Republic. In-person sites also in Chile, Mexico and Israel.
- Steps: confirm eligibility and consent online, complete a short survey, collect the sample at home, return it. Virtual appointments available. Contact Genetics@Parkinson.org.
- Yield: about 12 to 13 percent reportable (the Foundation FAQ says 12.2 percent; the 2026 preprint about 13), higher (about 18 percent) with early onset, high-risk ancestry or an affected first-degree relative. GBA1 is the most common finding, then LRRK2, then PRKN.
- Over 30,000 enrolled; about 70 percent had never joined research before. At-home uptake more than doubled since 2024.
- Sources: [Parkinson's Foundation FAQ](https://www.parkinson.org/advancing-research/our-research/pdgeneration/faq), [2026 preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353696v1.full), [NeurologyLive May 2026](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/expanding-access-parkinson-disease-research-through-at-home-genetic-testing), [AAN 2026 South Florida cohort](https://index.mirasmart.com/AAN2026/PDFfiles/AAN2026-003625.html).

### NHS genetic testing (UK)

England and Wales: clinicians in neurology, genetics or psychiatry can request testing for a person diagnosed before 50, for someone with a first-degree relative diagnosed before 50, or for complex features. Scotland uses its own directory with the same under-50 threshold. Northern Ireland aligns with England where possible. The exact R-code was not confirmed; check the current eligibility document (v9.1, May 2026). Source: [Parkinson's UK referral guidance](https://www.parkinsons.org.uk/professionals/resources/referring-people-parkinsons-for-genetic-testing), [NHS England test directories](https://england.nhs.uk/publication/national-genomic-test-directories).

### PD Frontline (UK research)

Finds people with LRRK2 and GBA1 variants for future trials. Questionnaire then saliva kit by post. Currently prioritising ages 35 to 75 diagnosed within the last 7 years. Research study, not an NHS service. Source: [Parkinson's UK](https://www.parkinsons.org.uk/node/1011021).

### Consumer kit (23andMe)

Reports two variants only: LRRK2 G2019S and GBA N370S (N409S under current nomenclature). Best studied in European, Ashkenazi Jewish and North African Berber ancestry. Both variants are rare in South Asian and East Asian populations, so a negative is even less informative there. Does not cover other genes or other variants in these two. Treat a positive as a lead to confirm with a clinical panel and counseling. Treat a negative as uninformative. Sources: [23andMe topic page](https://www.23andme.com/topics/health-predispositions/parkinsons-disease/), [sample report](https://medical.23andme.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Jamie-Parkinsons-Disease-one-variant-LRRK2-detected-sample-report-1.pdf).

### CSF seed amplification assay

- Product: SYNTap Biomarker Test, also listed as SAAmplify-αSYN (Amprion). CPT code 0393U (per [payer policy documents](https://www.premera.com/medicalpolicies/2.04.521.pdf)). FDA Breakthrough Device designation. Mayo Clinic Laboratories collaboration announced March 2025.
- Performance: in PPMI, 87.7 percent sensitivity and 96.3 percent specificity for clinical PD; much lower positivity in LRRK2 carriers and people with normal smell. Qualitative result only.
- Cost: a news report put self-pay at about $1,500; current price unverified. Medicare patients sign an Advance Beneficiary Notice. Not in network with private insurers as of the manufacturer FAQ; courtesy billing offered. Some Blue Cross policies list the Alzheimer's indication as investigational.
- Regulatory: FDA Letter of Support (2024) for use as a trial enrichment biomarker. No disease-modifying trial yet enrolls on synuclein status alone.
- Sources: [Amprion FAQ](https://www.ampriondx.com/test-faq), [Siderowf et al., Lancet Neurology 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00109-6), [Mayo collaboration](https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/2025/03/26/mayo-clinic-laboratories-and-amprion-announce-collaboration-to-advance-neurodegenerative-disease-diagnostics/).

### Skin biopsy

- Product: Syn-One Test (CND Life Sciences). Lab-developed test in a CLIA lab; not FDA cleared. Three 3 mm punch biopsies (neck or upper back, thigh, lower leg) under local anesthetic. Report in about three weeks.
- Performance: NIH-sponsored multicenter study (Gibbons, JAMA 2024): positive in 92.7 percent of PD, 98.2 percent MSA, 96.0 percent DLB; 3.3 percent of controls. Does not distinguish among synucleinopathies and does not give prognosis.
- Cost and coverage: not published; varies by payer.
- Sources: [CND Life Sciences](https://cndlifesciences.com/syn-one-test/), [JAMA 2024](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2816441).

### DaTscan

Ioflupane SPECT. Confirms presynaptic dopaminergic deficit, which is the "N" axis in both biological frameworks. Does not separate PD from atypical parkinsonism and does not subtype. Widely available in the US and Europe. PPMI provides it inside the study.

### Body-first vs brain-first

Research only. The clinical proxy an agent can ask about: did dream-enactment behavior (REM sleep behavior disorder) begin years before motor symptoms? If yes, body-first is likely, and polysomnography can confirm the sleep disorder. Cardiac sympathetic imaging and colon biopsy are not offered as clinical typing services. See `subtypes.md`.

### Prodromal screening

PPMI mails a scratch-and-sniff test in the US and Canada to people over 40 without a diagnosis, or over 30 and diagnosed within 7 years (expanded April 2025). Results entered online; some are invited to in-clinic assessment. Isolated REM sleep behavior disorder is the strongest single prodromal marker, converting at about 6 percent per year. Source: [MJFF PPMI page](https://michaeljfox.org/ppmi).

## Choosing between the skin biopsy and the spinal fluid test

The repo does not choose for a person. What it can say: the two tests look for the same biology by different methods. The spinal fluid assay amplifies misfolded synuclein seeds; the skin test stains nerve fibers for phosphorylated synuclein. Their published accuracy figures come from different studies and different cohorts, so 87.7 percent and 92.7 percent are not a head-to-head comparison. Neither test separates Parkinson's from multiple system atrophy or Lewy body dementia, which is often the actual question when a picture is "atypical." The practical differences are the lumbar puncture, the roughly three-week turnaround of the skin test, and billing: the spinal fluid test has a published self-pay price and a CPT code, the skin test does not publish a price. The questions to bring to the neurologist: which one their center can order, what they expect it to change, and whether genetic testing or a DaTscan should come first.

## What a negative synuclein result means

A negative result on either test does not mean a person does not have Parkinson's. In the PPMI cohort about one in eight people with a confident clinical diagnosis tested negative on the spinal fluid assay. Negatives are concentrated among LRRK2 and Parkin carriers and people with a normal sense of smell. Some negatives turn out to be a look-alike condition such as progressive supranuclear palsy, which is why the guide says a negative "prompts a diagnostic re-look." It is a reason to ask two questions, not a verdict: should I have genetic testing, and does this change your view of the diagnosis. Levodopa works regardless of synuclein status.

## What a result changes

See `subtypes.md` for the full treatment implications per axis. The short version:

- **GBA1 positive:** monitor cognition early; caution with anticholinergics and cognition-impairing drugs; discuss cognitive risk before deep brain stimulation; eligible for ASPro-PD (UK) and GREAT (Netherlands).
- **LRRK2 positive:** often slower course; about a third are synuclein-negative; the kinase inhibitor program for idiopathic PD ended in May 2026 and the carrier-only BEACON study is not recruiting. Relatives can consider testing.
- **PRKN or PINK1 positive:** young onset, slow course, strong levodopa response, good long-term surgical candidates.
- **Synuclein negative with clinical PD:** disproportionately LRRK2 or Parkin; anti-synuclein drugs unlikely to apply; diagnosis deserves a second look for tauopathy mimics.
- **Tremor-dominant:** surgical options work well, including focused ultrasound.
- **Gait-dominant or body-first features:** earlier cognitive screening, blood pressure management, fall prevention, exercise prescription.
